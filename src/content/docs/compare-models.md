---
title: Compare models with your voice
description: Read a few sentences and see which local model recognizes you best.
---

The best model depends on your voice, accent and microphone. This tool measures it on you.

## Run it

1. Download at least one local model (two or more to compare). Open **Settings → Speech → Local models**.
2. Click **Start…** next to "Compare models with my voice".
3. Read the five sentences, one at a time and at a normal pace: click **Record**, read, click **Stop**. You can redo any of them.
4. After three or more, click **Compare**.

Each downloaded model that covers your language recognizes the same recordings.

## Reading the result

- **Character error rate** is the share of characters that are wrong, missing or extra, ignoring punctuation. Lower is better.
- The model with the lowest rate is marked **Most accurate**. If two models differ by less than 1%, none is marked, because
  that is within the noise of a few sentences.
- **Average time per sentence** shows relative speed.
- A model that **writes no punctuation** is flagged, so a good accuracy score does not hide that you would add punctuation yourself.
- **Use this one** switches to that model.
- "By sentence" shows exactly what each model heard.

## Privacy

Recordings are kept in memory only. They are not written to disk and not uploaded, and they are cleared when you close the
window. Cloud services are not part of this comparison.
