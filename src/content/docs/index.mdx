<CardGrid stagger>
	<Card title="Private by design" icon="laptop">
		No account, no server, no analytics. With local recognition your audio never leaves the Mac. Cloud services receive audio only after your explicit consent for that provider, and a "never go online" switch hides everything that could connect.
	</Card>
	<Card title="Local models you can compare" icon="magnifier">
		SenseVoice, FireRedASR2 and Parakeet run on your Mac. "Compare models with my voice" shows which one suits you; your recordings stay in memory.
	</Card>
	<Card title="Your cloud service, optionally" icon="random">
		Bring your own account for iFLYTEK, Volcengine, Tencent Cloud, Alibaba Cloud, Baidu or Deepgram. If the network fails, a local model can take over.
	</Card>
	<Card title="Works with your hardware" icon="setting">
		An optional, off-by-default interface on 127.0.0.1 lets your own pendant, glasses or button send audio and receive text.
	</Card>
	<Card title="Open and measured" icon="open-book">
		Every change to recognition is justified by a repeatable benchmark. Read how it works, or help improve it.
	</Card>
	<Card title="Made together" icon="heart">
		MIT licensed, with a contributing guide, a code of conduct and a private way to report security problems.
	</Card>
